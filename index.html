About

Art Without
Boundaries

No Discipline Art is a Košice-based creative studio pushing the limits of metal as a medium. We believe great art follows no single rule — only raw instinct and refined craft.

From sculptural installations to bespoke interior pieces, every work is conceived, shaped, and finished entirely by hand in our workshop in the heart of eastern Slovakia.

Process

How We Create

01

Concept

Every piece begins as a raw idea — a tension, a shape, a contradiction worth exploring in metal.

02

Material

We select metals for their character — the way they age, reflect, oxidise, and hold light.

03

Craft

Hand-cut, welded, ground and shaped in our Košice studio. No shortcuts, no machines doing our thinking.

04

Finish

The surface is the soul. Whether raw, polished or patinated — every finish is deliberate.
